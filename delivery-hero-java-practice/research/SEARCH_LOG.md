# Search method and limits

Research date: 2026-09-27. This was a bounded public-web search, not access to an internal question bank.

## Search passes

1. Broad discovery: `Delivery Hero senior software engineer interview coding questions Java Berlin` and `Delivery Hero interview leetcode coding questions`.
2. Primary candidate accounts: `site:leetcode.com/discuss "Delivery Hero" "Coding question"`, the `DeliveryHero` spelling, and Java/Go/backend role variants.
3. Recency: coding/interview queries scoped to 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026. Newer search hits frequently describe a process without giving exact tasks; their dates were not used to make old questions appear new.
4. Pattern probes: LRU, linked list, pair/sum, binary search, substring, cache, Codility and practical REST tasks.
5. Cross-checking: opened detailed candidate reports, compared search excerpts with body text, and retained role/location/date limitations.
6. Employer context: read an official senior Java/Kotlin Fintech listing. Graduate-program guidance was found but not generalized to senior backend hiring.

## Evidence decisions

- Deduplicated reposted LeetCode accounts and Glassdoor country mirrors; mirrors are not independent corroboration.
- Word counting appears in two distinct Berlin candidate accounts. That is corroboration of reports, not a measured company frequency.
- Retained original links and summarized only the relevant evidence. No paid/private material was accessed, and no interview prompts were inferred from undisclosed assessments.
- Commercial company-tag pages were discovered, including InterviewSolver, PracHub, InterviewQuery, Jobmentis and DataLemur. Their generated practice prompts, unsourced frequency percentages, mixed-role question banks and editorial recommendations were not promoted to verified interview questions.
- Code-oriented anonymous reports can still be inaccurate or outdated. An explicit problem named by a candidate is stronger evidence than a page containing a company tag.
- Related Glovo, Foodpanda, automation and data-engineering examples were not silently merged into the Berlin senior backend category.

## What remains unknown

The user's exact team, job description, interview date, current round format, approved language, and present-day question pool. Ask the recruiter whether the round is DSA, pair programming, an API exercise or take-home, and whether a local IDE is allowed. The workbook remains usable without those answers.

We cannot claim to have found every historical question. Pages behind authentication, removed posts, undisclosed prompts and team-specific assignments remain outside this collection. Repeating searches across country mirrors adds little evidence; focused practice is the next useful step.

## Second pass: broadened scope

At the user's request, removed the geography restriction and searched all locations/roles plus foodpanda, Glovo, HungerStation and Talabat. Queries covered company-specific Glassdoor role pages and individual questions, and task terms including array, string, SQL, cache, sorting, flattening and design patterns. Also searched LeetCode Discuss and candidate accounts beyond Glassdoor.

Opened Delivery Hero senior software engineer, senior Python and senior React pages, Android question entries, the foodpanda principal set-design post, the Glovo Dinner Plate Stacks post, and the official hiring page. Cross-checked Glassdoor mixed-company results to avoid attributing nearby Meta/Mobiquity questions to Delivery Hero. Repeated country mirrors were not counted as independent reports.

Results and changes are recorded in [SECOND_PASS.md](SECOND_PASS.md), with S10–S22 added to the source ledger. Lower-confidence titles can now contribute as recommendations rather than being excluded entirely. Current sources vary in accessibility and date; this search did not inspect every historical Glassdoor page or private question bank.
